Who I Am

Hi! I'm John Carry Dogmoc, a web development student based in the Philippines with a strong interest in both front-end design and database-driven back-end systems. I plan user interfaces in Figma and then build them on the web—from simple personal pages to structured, data-focused systems.

I'm currently studying and continuously improving my skills in web development, focusing on turning detailed Figma layouts into functional, responsive interfaces using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while also strengthening my skills in PHP and MySQL for database work.

What I Do

I focus on front-end development and design, using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to translate my Figma designs into clean and accurate interfaces. In addition, I work with PHP and MySQL to design and manage databases that support the functionality of my projects.
